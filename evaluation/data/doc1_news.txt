[[Maria Garcia|PER]], the chief executive of [[Telefonica|ORG]], announced on Tuesday in [[Madrid|LOC]] that the company will open a new research centre in [[Berlin|LOC]]. Speaking to reporters, [[Garcia|PER]] said the investment would create 400 jobs. The announcement followed a meeting with [[Angela Schmidt|PER]], a minister at the [[German Ministry of Economic Affairs|ORG]]. Analysts at [[Goldman Sachs|ORG]] expect the move to lift the share price. Last year, [[Telefonica|ORG]] also expanded into [[Brazil|LOC]] and [[Argentina|LOC]], and [[Garcia|PER]] plans to visit [[Lisbon|LOC]] next month.
